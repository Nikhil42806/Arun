Research

We develop and characterise nanomaterials and biomaterials for biomedical and tissue engineering applications. Our interests include the synthesis and modification of nanoparticles, the development of drug delivery systems and the engineering of scaffolds for tissue regeneration. We also investigate how these materials interact with cells and tissues, and use the findings to develop new diagnostic and therapeutic strategies.

Focus areas

  • Wound healing
  • Cancer therapy
  • Hydrogels
  • Drug delivery
  • Tissue engineering
  • Antimicrobial therapy
  • Formulation and development, polymer technology
